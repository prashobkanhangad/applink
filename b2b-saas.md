## Universal B2B SaaS SEO/AEO Principles

Apply these to every piece of advice:

### 1. ICP-First, Not Keyword-First
Before any keyword or content decision, establish:
- Who is the primary buyer? (title, company size, industry)
- What is their job-to-be-done?
- What questions do they ask at each stage: Unaware → Problem Aware → Solution Aware → Purchase Ready?
- What objections block them from buying?

### 2. The 100-Word Rule (Non-Negotiable for AEO)
Every page must place a complete, direct answer to its primary question within the **first 100 words**.
AI answer engines extract the first clear answer. If it's buried in paragraph 7, it doesn't exist.

**Bad:** Background → context → industry trends → eventual answer
**Good:** Direct answer in sentence 1-3 → supporting detail → expansion

### 3. Pillar-Cluster Architecture
B2B SaaS SEO requires topic authority, not just individual rankings.
- **Pillar page**: Broadest version of a core topic (2,000-5,000 words, comprehensive)
- **Cluster pages**: Specific subtopics that link back to pillar (800-2,000 words, targeted)
- **Rule**: Every cluster links to its pillar. Every pillar links to all its clusters.
- **B2B SaaS clusters**: Definition pages, comparison pages, integration pages, use-case pages, ROI/pricing pages

### 4. Entity Authority Over Keyword Stuffing
AI answer engines understand entities, not just keywords. For every major concept:
- Link to Wikidata/Wikipedia where appropriate
- Build entity profile pages on your site
- Use `sameAs` properties in schema to declare entity relationships
- Reference authoritative external sources to signal context

### 5. E-E-A-T for SaaS
**Experience**: Customer stories, product screenshots, real usage data
**Expertise**: Detailed how-tos, technical depth, author credentials
**Authoritativeness**: Citations by others, press mentions, backlinks
**Trustworthiness**: Accurate pricing, honest comparisons, real reviews

### 6. Extractability = Citeability
Structure every page so an AI can extract a clean answer without seeing the whole page:
- Use H2/H3 headings as question labels ("What is X?", "How does X work?")
- Use tables for comparisons (AI models love structured data)
- Use numbered lists for steps (triggers HowTo schema)
- Use bullet points for features/benefits
- Add FAQ sections at the end of every substantive page

## Quick Prompts for AI Search (Apply to Your Brand)

These competitive intelligence prompts are battle-tested for B2B SaaS. Use them as templates:

1. **Visibility Gap**: Compare [your brand]'s AI visibility vs [competitor] across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini over last 90 days. Where do they outperform? What content drives it?
2. **Citation Audit**: Which third-party domains cite [competitor] in AI responses? Group by type. For top 10, what got them cited?
3. **Share of Voice**: Full share of voice comparison for last 30 days, broken down by AI platform, prompt category, topic.
4. **Prompt Blind Spot**: Which prompts does [competitor] appear in that you don't? What's their average mention position?
5. **Sentiment Advantage**: How do AI platforms describe [competitor] vs you? What language patterns are associated with them?
6. **Topic Ownership Map**: Every topic where [competitor] has AI visibility and you don't. Prioritise by search relevance.
